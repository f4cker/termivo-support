Privacy Policy · 个人测试版本 / Personal beta

支持 / Support隐私 / Privacy

更新日期 / Updated: 2026-10-13

本政策说明 LAVAS LABS LIMITED 提供的 Amber 如何处理信息。隐私问题请联系 reallayoute@gmail.com。本政策适用于 0.1.0 (2) 及其后的当前个人测试版本；构建 1 的数据库密码仅存本机，且没有接通 AI 服务。

本机配置、查询与凭据

连接名称、主机、端口、用户名、数据库名称及连接选项保存在应用的本机存储中。数据库密码使用系统钥匙串保存，配置文件不包含密码。0.1.0 (2) 起，数据库密码使用允许同步的钥匙串属性，系统可按你的 Apple 账户及 iCloud 钥匙串设置在兼容设备间同步。旧本机密码会尝试迁移；迁移失败或冲突时会保留可用密码并提示。App 无法确认跨设备送达进度。0.1.0 (4) 起，SSH 密码与支持格式的私钥也使用允许同步的独立系统钥匙串服务；代理密码及 AI API 密钥保存在独立的仅此设备钥匙串，不参与同步。SSH 跳板设置、已信任主机指纹及代理配置保存在本机；连接配置仍需在各设备单独设置。SSH 密钥管理页仅在你明确操作时复制或分享公钥，私钥保留在系统钥匙串，公钥副本由你和接收者管理。收藏查询、SQL 历史和草稿存于本机，可能包含你输入的业务信息或 SQL 字面值；查询库文件标记为不参与系统备份。

当前版本不提供连接配置、查询库或数据库结果的 Amber iCloud 同步；密码同步由系统 iCloud 钥匙串负责。并非所有本机文件都被排除于系统备份；设备备份、系统钥匙串及 Apple 服务遵循你的系统设置与 Apple 政策。卸载应用不保证钥匙串条目、系统备份或你自行保存的文件同时删除。

你选择连接的数据库

Amber 根据你的操作向你指定的数据库服务器传输认证信息、查询或已确认的变更、导入数据及必要的协议与网络数据，并接收结构信息、查询结果和服务器状态。远程查询结果会在设备上处理和展示。Amber 不把这些数据库凭据、查询或结果主动上传到开发者的数据库服务。

数据库运营者及其网络服务可能记录连接、查询和相关活动，并按其政策保留数据。请仅连接你信任且获授权使用的数据库，并按需要启用 TLS 和最小权限账号。TLS 默认完全校验；你可明确选择关闭 TLS，包括 PROD 连接，这会取消数据库协议本身的 TLS 保护，应用不会因连接失败自动降级。SSH 与 SOCKS5 是网络路径选项，不代替数据库 TLS。

SSH 隧道、代理与主动复制

0.1.0 (4) 起可按你的操作通过 SSH 单跳/多跳隧道，或 SOCKS5 代理访问你指定的数据库。SSH 服务器接收逐跳认证与转发目标，代理接收认证信息和连接目标；这些服务器及网络运营者可能记录连接活动。首次 SSH 主机指纹需要你核对并明确接受，指纹改变会阻断连接。SSH 与 SOCKS5 当前不能组合使用；HTTP CONNECT、Wi-Fi 自动代理与 SFTP 文件获取尚未接入。

你主动复制查询结果时，内容进入系统剪贴板，可按应用设置附带到期时间，由系统处理；系统连续互通与接收应用遵循你的系统设置及其政策。到期不删除已经粘贴、导出或被其他应用保存的副本。结果导出支持 CSV/JSON，脱敏规则当前只是本机草稿与预览，不自动脱敏复制或导出的真实记录。

文件导入、导出与本地数据库

你通过系统文件选择器授权 Amber 读取所选文件。应用会解析导入数据，按你的明确操作向目标数据库发送数据；本地 SQLite 文件会被读取并按功能需要制作工作副本。文件操作可能产生临时文件，写回失败等情况可能保留恢复用备份，并在界面提示。文件位置可能由你选择的云端文件提供者管理。

导出或分享的 CSV、JSON、SQL 等文件可能包含数据库记录、结构或查询内容，Amber 不自动对这些数据文件加密。选择保存位置、共享服务、接收者和删除导出文件由你决定；文件提供者或接收者可能按自己的政策保存副本。

系统权限与设备保护

本地网络权限用于连接局域网数据库。启用应用锁时，系统进行面容 ID 或设备密码验证，Amber 不接收或保存你的面容生物特征数据或设备密码。相机权限仅用于你主动进入的二维码扫描，图像在设备上处理，不用于拍照、保存或上传。通知权限用于应用中已提供的本机通知与测试通知；当前没有接入团队远程推送服务。

应用读取自己的偏好设置以提供外观、锁定与导航设置；按文件功能读取用户选择文件的元数据以检查变化，并在文件写入前检查可用磁盘空间。这些信息用于本机功能，不用于设备指纹识别或广告跟踪。

你选择的 AI 服务

AI 功能默认未配置，只有你填写自己的 HTTPS 服务地址、模型和 API 密钥并主动发送问题时，Amber 才会请求该服务。请求使用该服务的 API 密钥进行认证，并包含问题文本、数据库引擎、所选表名和表结构。0.1.0 (3) 起，连续追问还会发送当前对话中已成功完成的问题、回答与生成 SQL；更换服务配置或数据库引擎会清空对话历史。你主动使用 SQL 解释或改写时，会发送当前 SQL 文本、任务说明和数据库引擎，SQL 可能包含业务信息或字面值。不会自动附带数据库查询结果行或数据库登录密码；但你输入的问题、历史内容或 SQL 可能包含敏感内容，请核对后发送。查询结果图表在设备上生成，不因绘图上传结果。0.1.0 (4) 起，主动请求运维 AI 诊断时会发送选中慢语句/会话的引擎、采样时间、指标、采样限制、规范化执行计划摘要和经过字面值/注释处理的 SQL；不自动附带原始计划、服务器错误、连接凭据或结果行。SQL 标识符与指标仍可能包含业务信息，请核对后请求。服务返回的回答和建议 SQL 会在设备上展示，SQL 不会因收到回答而自动执行；改写结果需你明确应用至编辑器，再按正常只读查询流程手动运行。

API 地址和模型名称保存在本机偏好设置中；API 密钥按服务地址分别保存在专用本机钥匙串中，不进入连接配置、查询库、日志或 iCloud 钥匙串。请求不使用持久化 HTTP 缓存或 Cookie，也不跟随 HTTP 重定向转发请求。你可以在 AI 服务设置中移除当前服务的密钥，未配置时无法发送 AI 请求。所选服务提供者可能处理和保留问题、对话历史、SQL、表结构与回答；请在配置前查看其隐私政策、费用和数据保留规则。

团队、统计与广告

当前没有接入团队账号后端或团队网关。当前版本不包含广告、不进行跨应用广告跟踪，也不主动向开发者的统计服务发送使用数据。未来接入新的服务前，会更新本政策并说明相关数据流。

支持、TestFlight 与 Apple 服务

你主动发送支持邮件或反馈时，我们会使用你提供的邮箱、问题说明、截图和日志处理请求。请只提供必要且已脱敏的信息，不要发送密码、连接串、服务器地址或生产数据。支持信息在处理请求所需期间保留；可通过上述邮箱提出访问、更正或删除请求，适用法律或必要记录保存要求除外。

TestFlight 的安装、使用诊断、崩溃信息和 Beta 反馈由 Apple 根据你的设置及其政策处理，Apple 可能向开发者提供测试反馈和诊断信息。系统邮件、文件共享、备份等外部服务也按各自政策处理数据。当前测试版本没有应用内购买或订阅功能。

你的控制与删除

你可以在 Amber 删除连接及对应凭据、删除查询收藏或清除查询历史；也可在 AI 服务设置中移除当前服务密钥，或在系统设置中撤销已授予的权限。同步密码的删除和保留受系统 iCloud 钥匙串处理；App 不删除数据库服务器或 AI 提供者已经保存的数据。临时文件、文件备份及导出文件的保留取决于具体功能和保存位置，系统备份和钥匙串可能有独立的保留机制。数据库服务器上的记录需要通过数据库运营者或授权管理流程处理，删除本机连接不会删除服务器上的数据。

政策变更

数据处理方式变化时，我们会更新本页面和日期。隐私问题及请求请联系上述支持邮箱。

Privacy Policy

Amber is provided by LAVAS LABS LIMITED. Updated October 13, 2026. Contact: reallayoute@gmail.com. This policy applies to the current personal beta from 0.1.0 (2). Build 1 used device-only database credentials and had no connected AI service.

Local data and credentials

Connection settings, including database hosts and usernames, are stored on your device. Database passwords are not stored in connection configuration files. From 0.1.0 (2), they use synchronizable Keychain attributes; system iCloud Keychain may sync them across compatible devices according to your Apple account and settings. Legacy device-only items are migrated when possible; usable local passwords are retained on migration failures or conflicts. Amber cannot confirm delivery to another device. From 0.1.0 (4), SSH passwords and supported private keys also use a separate synchronizable system Keychain service. Proxy passwords and AI API keys remain in separate device-only Keychain services. SSH hop settings, accepted host fingerprints and proxy settings stay local; configure connection profiles separately on each device. The SSH key management page explicitly copies or shares public keys only; private keys remain in the system Keychain. Public-key copies are managed by you and their recipients. Saved SQL, history and drafts may contain business information or literal values; query-library files are excluded from system backup. This version does not provide Amber iCloud sync for connection profiles, query history or database results; system iCloud Keychain handles credential synchronization. Other files, Keychain items and system backups may follow separate retention rules; uninstalling the app does not guarantee their deletion.

Databases and files you choose

Your actions send authentication information, queries, confirmed changes, import data and protocol data directly to the database servers you select. Results and schema information are processed and displayed on your device. Amber does not proactively upload this database content or credentials to a developer-hosted database service. Server and network operators may log activity. Use trusted, authorized databases, TLS and least-privilege accounts. Full TLS verification is the default; explicitly selecting disabled TLS, including for PROD profiles, removes database-protocol TLS protection. Connection failures never automatically downgrade TLS.

Selected files are accessed through system-granted file permissions. Imports may send selected data to the target database after your confirmation. Local SQLite operations may create working copies; temporary or recovery files may be retained when an operation fails. Exported or shared CSV, JSON and SQL files may contain database content and are not automatically encrypted by Amber. You control their destinations and recipients; cloud file providers and recipients follow their own policies.

SSH, proxies and copied results

From 0.1.0 (4), your actions can use single/multi-hop SSH tunnels or SOCKS5 proxies. SSH servers receive hop authentication and forwarding targets; proxies receive authentication and destination metadata. Those operators may log connections. First-use SSH fingerprints require explicit acceptance, and changed fingerprints block connection. SSH and SOCKS5 cannot currently be combined; HTTP CONNECT, automatic Wi-Fi proxy selection and SFTP downloads are not connected. These routes do not replace database TLS.

Explicitly copying query results places content in the system clipboard with an optional expiration handled by the system. Continuity and receiving apps follow system settings and their policies. Expiration does not remove content already pasted, exported or saved elsewhere. CSV/JSON result exports are not automatically encrypted or masked. Masking rules currently provide only local drafts and supplied-value previews.

Permissions and services

Local-network permission supports LAN connections. App lock uses system Face ID or device-passcode verification; Amber does not receive biometric templates or the device passcode. Camera access is for on-device QR scanning, not saving or uploading photos. Notification permission supports currently available local/test notifications. Preferences, selected-file metadata and free disk space are accessed for app settings and file operations, not fingerprinting.

AI services you configure

AI is unconfigured by default. After you supply your own HTTPS API endpoint, model and API key and send a question, Amber authenticates to that provider with its key and sends your question, database engine, selected table names and schema. From 0.1.0 (3), follow-up requests also include completed questions, replies and generated SQL from the current conversation; changing the service configuration or database engine clears that history. When you request SQL explanation or rewriting, the current SQL text, task instructions and database engine are sent to the provider. Questions, history and SQL literals may contain sensitive content. Database result rows and database login credentials are not automatically included. Result charts are generated on-device without uploading rows for charting. From 0.1.0 (4), an explicit operations-AI diagnosis sends selected slow-statement/session facts: engine, sample time, metrics and collection limitations, normalized plan summaries and SQL after literal/comment redaction. Raw plans, remote errors, connection credentials and result rows are not automatically attached. Identifiers and metrics may still contain business information; review the context before requesting diagnosis. Replies and suggested SQL are shown locally; rewrites require your explicit application to the editor, and SQL is never automatically executed.

Endpoint/model settings stay in local preferences. API keys are stored separately for each endpoint in a device-only Keychain service, not in connection files, query history, logs or iCloud Keychain. Requests use no persistent HTTP cache or cookies and are not forwarded through HTTP redirects. Remove the current service key in AI settings to disable its requests. The provider may process and retain prompts, conversation history, SQL, schema and responses; review its privacy, retention and pricing policies before configuring it.

The current version has no team backend or gateway, no profile/history cloud sync, no advertising, no cross-app tracking and no proactive developer analytics uploads. It has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. We will update this policy before connecting new services.

Support, Apple and your choices

If you contact support, we use the email, description and redacted attachments you provide to handle your request. Keep credentials and production data out of reports. Information is retained as needed for support and applicable record-keeping; contact us to request access, correction or deletion. Apple processes TestFlight installation, diagnostics, crashes and beta feedback under its policies and your settings, and may share reports with developers. Other services, including email, backup and sharing providers, apply their own policies.

You can delete local connections and associated credentials, remove saved queries or clear query history, remove the current AI service key, and revoke system permissions. System iCloud Keychain manages deletion/retention of syncable passwords. Amber does not delete information already held by a database or AI provider. Files, backups and Keychain items may have separate retention. Deleting a local connection does not delete database-server data. Contact the support address above for privacy questions.

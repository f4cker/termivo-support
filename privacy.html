本政策说明 LAVAS LABS LIMITED 提供的 Termivo 如何处理应用使用过程中的信息。联系方式：reallayoute@gmail.com。

本机配置与凭据

服务器地址、连接配置、命令和巡检计划保存在你的设备上。密码与 SSH 私钥由系统钥匙串保护。Termivo 不要求创建独立账号，当前版本不将连接配置或凭据同步至 iCloud。系统备份及 Apple 服务的处理方式由你的系统设置与 Apple 政策决定。

你选择连接的服务

使用 SSH、SFTP、代理或端口转发时，应用会按照你的操作向指定服务器传输认证信息、命令、文件及必要的网络数据，并接收服务器响应。服务的运营者可能记录这些活动，请仅连接你信任且获授权使用的服务。

自托管巡检与通知

配置巡检器后，应用与所选巡检器交换授权信息、服务器状态、计划及报告。启用远程推送时，设备推送令牌、通知环境及推送订阅信息会发给你配置的推送中继，由中继通过 Apple 推送通知服务发送通知。巡检器与中继的数据保留由其运营者决定，你可以撤销配对、关闭推送或删除相关配置。

购买与订阅

付款、订阅与退款由 Apple 处理。Termivo 使用 Apple 提供的已验证购买记录判断使用权限，不读取你的支付卡信息。你可以在 Apple 账户中管理订阅与购买。

统计与广告

当前版本不包含广告，也不将使用情况发送至开发者的统计服务，不进行跨应用广告跟踪。本机资源历史与连接状态用于提供应用功能。你主动通过邮件联系支持时，我们会使用邮件内容处理你的请求；请勿发送服务器密码、私钥或生产数据。

文件导出与删除

导出的配置文件受你设置的密码保护；保存、共享、备份和删除导出文件由你负责。你可以在应用内删除连接配置、命令和密钥，并清除巡检配对。系统钥匙串和系统备份可能有独立的保留机制，卸载应用不保证这些数据同时删除。

政策变更

如果数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页面及日期。与隐私相关的问题请发送至上述支持邮箱。

Privacy Policy

Termivo is provided by LAVAS LABS LIMITED. Contact: reallayoute@gmail.com.

Local settings and credentials

Server addresses, connection settings, commands and inspection plans are stored on your device. Passwords and SSH private keys are protected by the system Keychain. Termivo does not require a separate account and this version does not sync these settings or credentials through iCloud. System backups and Apple services depend on your system settings and Apple's policies.

Services you choose

SSH, SFTP, proxy and forwarding features send authentication information, commands, files and necessary network data to the servers you select, according to your actions. Their operators may keep logs. Connect only to services you trust and are authorized to use.

Self-hosted inspections and notifications

Configured collectors exchange authorization information, server status, plans and reports with the app. Remote notifications send your device push token, notification environment and subscription information to your configured relay, which delivers notifications through Apple's Push Notification service. Retention depends on the collector and relay operators. You can revoke pairing, disable push or remove the related settings.

Purchases

Apple handles payments, subscriptions and refunds. Termivo uses verified purchase records to determine access and does not receive payment card details. Manage subscriptions and purchases in your Apple account.

Analytics, advertising and support

This version has no ads, does not send usage data to a developer analytics service and does not track you across apps for advertising. Local resource history and connection state support app features. If you email support, we use the information you provide to handle your request. Do not send passwords, private keys or production data.

Exports and deletion

Configuration exports are protected by your chosen password. You control where you save, share, back up and delete them. Remove connection settings, commands, keys and collector pairings in the app. The system Keychain and backups have independent retention behavior; uninstalling the app does not guarantee their deletion.

Changes

We update this page and its date when data handling changes. Send privacy questions to the support address above.
